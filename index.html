    How to run this in class (for the teacher)
    1. Share your screen and read the story aloud.
    2. Start the timer, then reveal one clue at a time, roughly every 40 seconds.
    3. After each clue, ask students in the chat: "Who can we rule out, and why?" Cross out suspects as the class agrees.
    4. When the class is confident, pick the suspect and make the accusation. The answer shows the reasoning step by step.
    5. Close with one question: "Did anyone guess too early? What made you sure in the end?"
